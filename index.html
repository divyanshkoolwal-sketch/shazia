What it tracks

Your whole health in one bracelet
that actually looks like one.

The sensor reads your skin temperature and heart rate. The app turns that into five things you can use.

01Hot flashes

Tracks every hot flash.

It notices when a hot flash starts, then records how long it lasts and how strong it is. Day and night.

02Period

Tracks your period.

Log your period in the app with one tap. Over time it learns your cycle and tells you when the next one is likely.

03Treatment

Shows if your treatment works.

Tell the app what you are taking, like HRT, in your own words. It compares your hot flashes week by week, so you can see if they are getting better.

Writes a report for your doctor.

Before an appointment, get a one-page summary of your hot flashes, period, sleep and treatment to show your doctor.

sházia — also written Shazia — is a women’s health wearable: a gold-tone bracelet with a sensor hidden on the inside, made for perimenopause and menopause. It tracks hot flashes and your period, shows whether your treatment is working, writes a report for your doctor, and does everything a fitness tracker does.
